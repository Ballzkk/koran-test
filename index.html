Frequently Asked Questions

How do I solve the questions?

Add the two digits (top and bottom). If the result is 14, type '4'. If it is 8, type '8'.

Is registration required?

Not mandatory. You can practice as a Guest. Registering is recommended to save history and join leaderboards.

What is the difference between Quick and Standard mode?

Quick test lasts 120 seconds for rapid warmup. Standard test lasts 5 minutes simulating standard HR recruitment tests.

Is there a mobile app?

Koran Test is a PWA web application. You can "Add to Home Screen" via your mobile browser for an app-like experience.

Secrets to Passing Pauli & Kraepelin Career Tests

Speed calculation tests (Pauli/Kraepelin) are a staple in recruitment. Sheets filled with numbers often tire candidates and test their focus.

However, recruiters do not only look for who finishes fastest. Key evaluated dimensions include:

  • Speed: Measures your working pace.
  • Accuracy: Measures your error rate under pressure.
  • Consistency (Endurance): Your performance graph will be evaluated. Recruiters favor stable or steadily rising curves rather than sharp drops.

Therefore, practice not only for speed but also to maintain your rhythm. Use the Marathon Test feature on this platform to build endurance.
